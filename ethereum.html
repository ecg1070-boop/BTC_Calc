About Ethereum units

Ether (ETH) is Ethereum’s native currency. Wei is its smallest unit, and gwei is commonly used to quote the price per unit of gas. These are different ways to express the same currency, so no exchange rate is needed. Read Ethereum.org’s guide to ether.

How to use the calculator

  1. Enter a number in Amount. Commas and decimals are accepted.
  2. Choose the unit that number is already in from Input unit.
  3. Read the Converted amount for any unit. The highlighted row shows your input.

Try converting 25 gwei

Enter 25 and choose gwei (shannon). The same amount is 25,000,000,000 wei or 0.000000025 ETH.

If 25 gwei is a gas price, it is not the total fee. To find the total, multiply the price per unit of gas by the gas used. Read Ethereum.org’s guide to gas fees.
