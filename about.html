ABOUT THE CALCULATORS

How denomination conversions work

A denomination changes how you express an amount, not its value. Each calculator converts between units of one currency only.

Try two examples

Bitcoin
50,000 satoshi = 0.0005 BTC
Ethereum
25 gwei = 0.000000025 ETH

How to use the site

  1. Open the Bitcoin calculator or the Ethereum calculator.
  2. Enter an amount and select the unit it’s already in.
  3. Find your target unit in the results. The highlighted row shows the original input.

What the results mean

The calculators use fixed factors, not market prices. They don’t convert BTC to ETH, quote fiat prices, or calculate transaction fees.

Denomination conversions are exact for accepted inputs. The Bitcoin calculator marks rounded Algorithmic max ratios with ≈.
